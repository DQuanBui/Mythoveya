# The living world

Mythoveya's islands react to the time of day, the weather and to you. None of this costs or earns anything: it is there to watch and enjoy. Everything that moves holds still with **Reduced Motion**, and purely decorative effects can be turned down (see Settings below).

## Your companion

| When | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| You stand still beside it | Looks up at you; you glance down at it now and then. |
| You wave (1) | Hops happily. |
| You cheer (2) or dance (3) | Plays along, tail wagging hard. |
| You sit (4) | Settles low beside you, then dozes off. |
| You find something (a cache, a lantern, a gift, a catch, Sunseed) | Hops with joy for a couple of seconds. |
| You wait about 12 seconds | Stretches long and low, then curls up for a nap with Z's floating up. |
| It naps near Sunpetal Clearing | A butterfly may land on its head. |
| It stops after a walk in the rain | Shakes itself dry, flinging droplets. |
| It trots through a full puddle | Splashes. |
| You are in Whisperleaf | The pollen makes it sneeze now and then. |
| It is dark | Keeps closer to your lantern. |
| It is shimmering | Trails a few golden sparkles as it follows you. |

It also leaves paw prints beside your footprints in snow and sand.

## You

- **After dark** you carry a lantern that lights the ground around you, and a few fireflies drift over to circle it.
- **In the rain** you hold a broad leaf over your head. Stepping in a puddle splashes and leaves a few wet footprints.
- **In Moonfrost** your breath clouds in the cold, and on cold nights you hug yourself and shiver while standing about.
- **Sprinting** kicks up dust on paths and sand, and powder in snow.
- Each keeper has an idle habit: hands on hips, shading the eyes, a stretch, bouncing, folded arms, a hand to the chin, humming (with little notes), or a tapping foot.

## Villagers

- Villagers, hosts and rangers turn their heads to watch you pass.
- Wave and they wave back; cheer and they cheer along.
- Islanders strolling about stop to chat when they meet, with a speech bubble now and then.
- From dusk they carry lanterns home, and in the rain they shelter under leaves too.

## Wildlife

- Wild creatures stop to watch you with a "!" or "?", hop about when you are right beside them, and show a heart while they play.
- They sleep after dark, puffing Z's.
- Sparrows peck about the village green, the clearing and the orchard by day, and scatter when you walk up.
- Fish leap in Willowmere pond and Reedwater Lake.

## Weather and time

| Time or weather | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Dawn (about 5:30 to 9:30) | Mist lies low over Havenreach and Whisperleaf, and dew glitters on the grass. |
| Rain | Puddles fill on the trails and dry slowly afterwards; raindrops ring the ponds and lakes; grass, trees and chimney smoke lean in the wind; fireflies shelter; birds roost. |
| After rain | A rainbow may hang ahead of you while the sky clears. |
| Night | Fireflies (unless it rains), shooting stars on clear nights, lanterns in hands. |

Each region has its own air: embers rise in Emberglass, pollen drifts in Whisperleaf, fresh snow sparkles around you in Moonfrost, and every pond and lake glints in the sun.

## Battles

Creatures drop onto the field with a puff of dust. Each attacks in its family's style and turns to face its target, and skills fly along their element's own path. Ultimates gather power first. Hits throw element-shaped debris, bigger when an element strikes its weakness. Health bars leave a draining trail and pulse red when nearly empty. Statuses show on the creatures, and badly hurt ones pant and droop. Knocked-out creatures fall in a puff of dust with a short camera jolt. Winners turn round and celebrate under confetti.

## Settings

- **Sound & graphics → Decorative effects:** *Auto* (the default) pauses drifting motes, glints, puddles and puffs if the frame rate stays below about 30 fps, and brings them back once it recovers. *Full* always shows them; *Minimal* keeps them off.
- **Reduced Motion** stills animation everywhere, and **Camera shake** controls the battle jolts.
- **Esc → Controls** lists every shortcut. Outdoors, **E** uses whatever is within reach.
