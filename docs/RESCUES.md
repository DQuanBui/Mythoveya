# Wildbound Rescues

Your companions can help the world outside battle. Four field keepers need help making safe places for small travelers. Each incident has three tasks and a permanent ending choice.

Open **Adventure > Wildbound Rescues / Help wildlife with your companions**, Events, or the pause menu. On the **Travel map**, select a rescue clearing, choose **Set walking waypoint**, then **Walk to waypoint**.

## Meet the field keepers

| Region | Keeper | Clearing | Wildlife |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Havenreach | Mira | Willowrun nursery | Reedrake |
| Whisperleaf Meadow | Oren | Bramblefall crossing | Reedrake |
| Emberglass Canyon | Tavi | Sunspade dig camp | Solmole |
| Moonfrost Hollow | Edda | Snowbell shelter | Solmole |

Form your first six with Warden Liora. Rescues can be completed in any order, independently of the Atlas, Rally and Conservancy. Existing region gates apply: Emberglass opens after two battle wins, Moonfrost after five.

## Choose a companion who can help

At the keeper, select **Survey the rescue site**. Read the observation, then choose a companion from your active team for each task. The journal marks compatible helpers and explains every ability.

| Ability | Elements | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brace | Stone / Frost | Steadies frames, branches and stone awnings |
| Nurture | Tide / Grove | Refreshes wildlife and living plants |
| Beacon | Flame / Light | Warms shelters and lights routes |
| Scout | Storm / Shadow | Follows calls and tracks to hidden wildlife |

Your starting six cover all four abilities, regardless of your starter choice. You can use **Change active team** to try another companion. Each successful task gives its helper **2 friendship**, capped at 100. Helping consumes no currency, items, battle energy or recruitment chances. The journal remembers each helper's species and completed task.

The site changes as you help: frames straighten, plants recover, and lamps glow. After all three tasks, choose an ending:

- **Build a sheltered rest:** add a canopy and keep the wildlife near its keeper.
- **Keep an open wildlife route:** add trail flags and give the wildlife more room to wander.

Both give **70 Gold, 20 Diamonds and 25 XP**, once per site. The ending is permanent. These incidents have no daily timer or repeatable reward. Normal level-up bonuses can add Diamonds.

## Welcome new companions

At any regional rescue keeper, complete two rescues to **Welcome Reedrake**, then all four to **Welcome Solmole**. Each reward can be claimed once; owning the species already grants its normal duplicate shard without changing its shimmering appearance. Four rescues also earn the **Trailkeeper** HUD badge.

**Reedrake** is a B-tier Tide healer with a jade body, coral webbed wings, cattail horns and a turquoise paddle tail. **Reedwater Rest** heals allies and grants regeneration.

**Solmole** is an A-tier Flame controller with plum fur, golden spade claws, a coral star nose and amber lantern stones. **Sunspade Sweep** damages and weakens the selected enemy row.

Both also appear in normal recruitment. Tier odds are unchanged. The roster now contains 90 base species and 16 evolved forms.

## Saved progress and scope

Surveys, helpers, endings, friendship and reward claims persist on the server. The server checks region, mainland presence, active-team ownership, element, task sequence and reward eligibility. Replayed operation IDs cannot duplicate rewards. Physical distance is enforced by the client, as with the existing exploration stories.

These are personal saved rescue incidents within the four existing map boundaries. They are not shared multiplayer events. Field keepers use existing avatar bodies with field accessories; clearings are outdoor landmarks.
